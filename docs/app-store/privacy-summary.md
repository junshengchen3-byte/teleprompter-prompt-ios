# Privacy Summary

`提词器Prompt` stores scripts and settings locally on device.

The MVP does not require account login and does not upload scripts or videos to a server.

Permissions:

- Camera: used for in-app camera preview and video recording in `拍摄提词`.
- Microphone: used to record spoken audio in `拍摄提词`.
- Photos add access: used to save completed recordings to the system Photos library.

No analytics, cloud sync, third-party account integration, or remote storage is included in the MVP.
